# Prune, 2026-11-02

**The rule.** We follow Amp: "If we don't use and love a feature, we kill it." There is no
backcompat and nothing legacy. A feature that goes is deleted, not hidden in the palette or
behind a setting, and when it's unclear, it goes. ([Amp manual](https://ampcode.com/manual))

**The result.** Branch `prune` against `main`: 71 files changed, +601 and −3916 lines. There are
20 commits, one per area. `swift test` passes.
